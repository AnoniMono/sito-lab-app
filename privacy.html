Privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

What this page is

This is a demo of a product in development, run by Martina Cesarini and Filippo Rosati. It is not a commercial service and sells nothing.

What this page collects

Nothing. No forms, no visitor analytics, no advertising or tracking cookies.

  • The Studio demo runs entirely in your browser and keeps what you type in memory only: closing or reloading the page clears it.
  • The language you pick is remembered in your browser's local storage, and nowhere else.

The survey

The feedback survey is a Google Form, opened in a new tab. Google processes your answers under its own privacy policy. We read the answers to decide what to build.

The email field is optional. If you fill it in, we use it only to arrange the call or the trial you asked for, and delete it once it is no longer needed. To have it deleted sooner, reply to any message we send you.

Fonts

The text on this site uses IBM Plex, loaded from Google Fonts. When the page loads, your browser contacts Google's servers, which see your IP address.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to its use. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).
